It's Nicola. A while back you tapped "notify me" on the coming-soon panel
inside PlaceMate -- so this is the one heads-up I promised you.

You can now ask your own notes.

You know the feeling: someone shows you how to do something on placement,
you write it down, and three weeks later you're asked to do it again and
the order has gone completely. Instead of scrolling back through
everything, you can just ask -- in plain English, the way you'd ask a
friend:

  "I remember logging how to take a manual blood pressure, but I've
   forgotten the order of the steps"

and your own note comes straight back, word for word, exactly as you
wrote it. Not a summary, not someone else's version -- yours.

Where to find it: the Ask box at the top of any page, or on your Home
screen.

Two honest things, because this is new:

It only knows what you've written. If you haven't logged something, it
will tell you so rather than make something up -- and nudge you to
capture it next time you're on placement.

It's study support, not clinical guidance. It'll help you remember and
double-check your own learning, but your placement's local policy always
wins. Please treat it that way.

And because it's in beta, your questions and answers are stored and may
be read by us to make it better. Nothing else about your notes changes --
they're still yours.

It will get things wrong sometimes. There's a thumbs up and thumbs down
under every answer -- those go straight to us and are genuinely the
fastest way to make it better. Or just hit reply, as always.

Hope it saves you a bit of scrolling.
