You can now ask your own notes.

You know the feeling: someone shows you how to do something on placement, you write it down, and three weeks later you're asked to do it again and the order has gone completely. Instead of scrolling back through everything, you can just ask — in plain English, the way you'd ask a friend:

“I remember logging how to take a manual blood pressure, but I've forgotten the order of the steps”

and your own note comes straight back, word for word, exactly as you wrote it. Not a summary, not someone else's version — yours.

You'll find it in the Ask box at the top of any page, or on your Home screen.

Try it now →

Two honest things, because this is new.

It only knows what you've written. If you haven't logged something, it'll say so rather than make something up — and nudge you to capture it next time you're on placement.

It's study support, not clinical guidance. It'll help you remember and double-check your own learning, but your placement's local policy always wins. Please treat it that way.

While it's in beta 🌱 — your questions and the answers are stored, and we may read them to make it better. Nothing else about your notes changes; they're still yours.

It will get things wrong sometimes. There's a 👍 and 👎 under every answer — those come straight to us and are honestly the fastest way to make it better. Or just hit reply, as always.

Hope it saves you a bit of scrolling.
