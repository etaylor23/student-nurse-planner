It's Nicola. There's something new in PlaceMate, and it's the start of
something we've been calling PlaceMate Intelligence. Two features, built
on one simple idea: your notes should work harder for you than a
notebook ever could. The first one is live today.

You can now ask your own notes.

You know the feeling. Someone shows you how to do something on
placement, you write it down, and three weeks later you're asked to do
it again and the order has gone completely. Instead of scrolling back
through everything, you can just ask, in plain English, the way you'd
ask a friend:

  "I remember logging how to take a manual blood pressure, but I've
   forgotten the order of the steps"

and your own note comes straight back, word for word, exactly as you
wrote it. Not a summary, not someone else's version. Yours.

You'll find it in the Ask box at the top of any page, or on your Home
screen.

Try it now: https://app.placemate.uk/

Two honest things, because this is new.

It only knows what you've written. If you haven't logged something,
it'll say so rather than make something up, and it'll nudge you to
capture it next time you're on placement.

It's study support, not clinical guidance. It'll help you remember and
double-check your own learning, but your placement's local policy
always wins. Please treat it that way.

While it's in beta: your questions and the answers are stored, and we
may read them to make it better. Nothing else about your notes changes;
they're still yours.

It will get things wrong sometimes. There's a thumbs up and thumbs down
under every answer, and those come straight to us. Honestly the fastest
way to make it better. Or just hit reply, as always.

COMING NEXT, and I'm genuinely excited about this one

The other half of PlaceMate Intelligence. Take a photo of your
handwritten notes and PlaceMate turns them into real entries (skills,
hours, reflections) without you retyping a word of it. All the scribbles
from a long shift, captured in the time it takes to snap a picture.
We're aiming to have it with you in the next week or two.

Hope it saves you a bit of scrolling.
