Write them, snap them, save them, search them.

Your notes, that is. Scribble on placement the way you always have. At the end of the day, tap the Photo button at the top of the app and snap the page. That's the whole job.

While you put the kettle on, PlaceMate:

reads the page twice, so a misread doesn't get to look right. Anything the readings don't agree on is flagged worth a check before it goes anywhere near your records
tidies the clinical spelling. Drug names and clinical terms only, never your wording, never your abbreviations
works out where each note belongs: medication log, skills, reflections, NMC evidence or shift notes

Then it hands the page back to you, note by note. Nothing is saved until you say so. File a note in one tap, fix a word in one tap, bin the bits that were never meant to be records. We've all written a shopping list in the margin.

Your drawings survive too. And they're searchable.

Sketch a mind map in a lecture, or a flowchart of what to do when a patient's blood sugar drops, and PlaceMate doesn't flatten it into text. It redraws it as a clean diagram, keeps it with your photographed page, and files it on the shift it came from.

The sketch on your page · the same flowchart, redrawn

And because it's saved properly, you can ask for it back: weeks later, type “show me that sepsis mind map” and there it is, redrawn and readable, with a link to the shift you learned it on. Revision scribbles used to live and die in one notebook. Now they're a library.

Two honest things, because this one matters.

Before you snap, check nothing patient-identifiable is in frame. No names, NHS numbers or dates of birth, and no other paperwork behind your page. The app reminds you every single time, on purpose. This one is on all of us to get right.

It will still misread the odd word. Late-shift handwriting is a challenge for anyone. That's why you review every note before it files, why disputed words are flagged worth a check, and why your photo stays alongside, so you can always compare against what you actually wrote.

While it's in beta 🌱  your photos are stored so you can check them against what we read, and the PlaceMate team may review them to make the reading better. Everything you file is still yours, exactly as before.
