Take a photo of your handwritten notes, and they become real entries.

Picture the end of a long day: a page full of scribbles — a drug you
looked up, a skill someone walked you through, half a reflection, a
to-do in the margin. Instead of retyping any of it, tap the Photo
button at the top of the app and snap the page. That's the whole job.

While you put the kettle on, PlaceMate:

  - reads the page twice — two readers, so anything they disagree on
    gets flagged for you rather than slipping through
  - tidies the clinical spelling — drug names and clinical terms only,
    never your wording or your abbreviations
  - works out where each note goes — medication log, skills,
    reflections, NMC evidence or shift notes

Then it hands the page back to you, note by note. Nothing is saved
until you say so — you file each note with one tap, fix a word with one
tap, or bin the bits that were never meant to be records (we've all
written a shopping list in the margin).

Photograph a page: https://app.placemate.uk/

My favourite part: if you've drawn something — a mind map from a
lecture, a flowchart of what to do when a patient's blood sugar drops —
PlaceMate redraws it as a proper diagram and keeps it with the page,
right on the shift it belongs to. Your revision scribbles, kept whole.

Two honest things, because this one matters.

Before you snap, check nothing patient-identifiable is in frame — no
names, NHS numbers, dates of birth, or other people's paperwork behind
your page. The app will remind you every single time, on purpose. This
one is on all of us to get right.

It will misread a word now and then — handwriting at the end of a long
shift is a challenge for anyone. That's why you review every note
before it's filed, why anything the two readers disagreed on is flagged
"worth a check", and why your photo stays alongside so you can always
compare against what you actually wrote.

While it's in beta: your photos are stored so you can check them
against what we read, and the PlaceMate team may review them to make
the reading better. Everything you file is still yours, exactly as
before.
