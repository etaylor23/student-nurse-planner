Write them, snap them, save them, search them.

Your notes, that is. Keep scribbling on placement the way you always
have; from there it's three steps, and two of them happen while you
put the kettle on:

  1. SNAP THE PAGE. Tap the Photo button, point it at your notes.
     That's your part done.

  2. PLACEMATE READS IT TWICE. A misread never gets to look right:
     anything the readings don't agree on is flagged "worth a check"
     before it goes anywhere near your records.

  3. YOU FILE EACH NOTE WITH ONE TAP. Nothing is saved until you say
     so. Fix a word in a tap, bin the shopping list in the margin.

  4. AND FROM THAT ONE PHOTO, YOU GET ALL OF THIS:
     - every note filed where it belongs: medication log, skills,
       reflections, NMC evidence or shift notes
     - clinical skills and reflections auto-tagged, with the NMC
       proficiencies they could evidence lined up ready
     - clinical spelling tidied. Drug names and terms only, never
       your wording
     - all of it searchable from the moment you file it. That last
       one is my favourite; more in a second

YOUR DRAWINGS SURVIVE TOO. AND THEY'RE SEARCHABLE.

Sketch a mind map in a lecture, or a flowchart of what to do when a
patient's blood sugar drops, and PlaceMate doesn't flatten it into
text. It redraws it as a clean diagram, keeps it with your
photographed page, and files it on the shift it came from. And because
it's saved properly, you can ask for it back: weeks later, type "show
me that sepsis mind map" and there it is, redrawn and readable, with a
link to the shift you learned it on. Revision scribbles used to live
and die in one notebook. Now they're a library.

Two honest things before you start snapping.

Before you snap, check nothing patient-identifiable is in frame. No
names, NHS numbers or dates of birth, and no other paperwork behind
your page. The app reminds you every single time, on purpose. This one
is on all of us to get right.

It will still misread the odd word. Late-shift handwriting is a
challenge for anyone. That's why nothing files without you, and why
your photo stays alongside so you can always check against what you
actually wrote.

While it's in beta: your photos are stored so you can check them
against what we read, and the PlaceMate team may review them to make
the reading better. Everything you file is still yours, exactly as
before.
